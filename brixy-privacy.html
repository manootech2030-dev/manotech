1. Scope & Game Identity

This Privacy Policy applies solely to the Android casual mobile game Brixy (Application ID: com.aistudio.brixy.kwxyzq), developed, operated, and published by MANOO Tech ("we", "our", or "the Studio").

Brixy is an arcade-style casual match-3 puzzle game featuring colorful sweets, jelly drops, cascade combos, dynamic power-up boosters (such as rockets, bombs, and hammers), and hundreds of handcrafted puzzle levels running at a silky 60 FPS.

By installing, updating, or playing Brixy, you acknowledge the terms described in this policy. If you do not agree with any terms, please discontinue use of the game.

2. Privacy by Design & Zero Personal Data Sale

Brixy is engineered strictly under a Privacy-by-Design philosophy. You do not need to create an account, register, log in with social media, or disclose any personal identity (such as your full name, email, phone number, address, or payment details) to play the game.

Zero Personal Data Sale: We do not collect, log, sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal information, address books, location coordinates, photos, or personal files to data brokers, advertising aggregators, or third parties.

3. Android Permissions & Hardware Usage

Brixy requests only the absolute minimum runtime permissions required to deliver an immersive casual gaming experience:

  • VIBRATE (Haptic Feedback): Used purely on-device to trigger short, tactile vibrations when you match candies, detonate explosive boosters (rockets/bombs), or clear levels. You can disable haptic feedback at any time within the game settings menu.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used strictly to verify app licensing and integrity via Google Play Services, download periodic game updates, and load contextual, family-safe advertisements (if applicable).
  • WAKE_LOCK: Used to prevent your phone screen from dimming or falling asleep while actively playing an ongoing puzzle stage.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: Brixy does not request access to your device contacts, precise GPS location, microphone, camera, or external storage files.

4. On-Device Storage & Gameplay Progression

All data associated with your gameplay is stored locally inside the application's isolated sandboxed storage on your device:

  • Stage Progression: Levels completed, high scores, and stars earned (1–3 stars).
  • Inventory & Boosters: Collected hammers, rockets, explosive bombs, and board-clearing power-ups.
  • Preferences: Sound effects volume, background music toggle, and haptic feedback settings.
  • Offline Playability: Because progression is saved locally, Brixy is fully playable offline without requiring an active internet connection.

5. Advertising, Analytics & Third-Party Services

To support free access and maintain performance stability, Brixy may integrate certified Google developer SDKs:

  • Google Play Services: Used for in-app updates, platform security verification, and app integrity. Subject to the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Family-Safe Advertising: Any in-game ads served (such as rewarded video ads to earn bonus boosters) strictly adhere to Google Play Families Policy guidelines. Ads are contextual and vetted to ensure no inappropriate or mature content is ever shown.
  • No Behavioral Tracking for Minors: We prohibit behavioral advertising or cross-app interest tracking for children. You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID anytime via Android Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Anonymous Diagnostics: Aggregated, anonymous crash telemetry (such as device model, OS build, and stack trace logs) helps us fix bugs and prevent frame drops without identifying individual players.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Brixy is designed as a family-friendly game appealing to players of all ages. We take children's privacy with the utmost seriousness:

  • COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance: We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation for Kids (GDPR-K).
  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not knowingly collect, request, or store personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).
  • Safe Ad Environment: In compliance with Google Play Families Policy, all ad SDKs utilized in Brixy are certified and configured with tagForChildDirectedTreatment to prevent personalized tracking.
  • Parental Rights: If a parent or guardian believes their child has submitted personal details to us without consent, please contact us immediately at manootech2030@gmail.com, and we will promptly purge such data.

7. User Rights & Data Deletion Mechanism

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policy and GDPR / CCPA privacy frameworks:

  • Resetting Game Data Locally: You can wipe all stored level progress, star ratings, and preferences at any time by going to Android Settings > Apps > Brixy > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application.
  • Support Records Deletion: If you have sent support emails or queries to our developer address and wish to have all correspondence permanently removed from our mail servers:
Submit a Deletion Request: Send an email with subject "Data Deletion Request - Brixy" to manootech2030@gmail.com. All associated email correspondence will be permanently purged within 48 business hours.

8. Data Security & Technical Safeguards

We prioritize your digital safety with industry-standard technical measures:

  • Encrypted Connections: Any technical communications initiated by Brixy (such as update checks) utilize modern encryption protocols (HTTPS / TLS 1.3).
  • Application Sandboxing: Android's application isolation architecture prevents unauthorized third-party apps from reading or modifying Brixy's local save files.

9. Intellectual Property & Fair Play

All graphical assets, candy illustrations, fruit icons, animations, booster mechanics, sound effects, logos, and code related to Brixy are the proprietary intellectual property of MANOO Tech.

You are granted a personal, non-commercial, revocable license to install and play the game via official distribution channels (such as the Google Play Store). Decompilation, extraction of artistic assets, or unauthorized redistribution is strictly prohibited.
