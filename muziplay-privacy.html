1. Scope & Application Identity

This Privacy Policy applies solely to the Android application MuziPlay (Application ID: com.aistudio.muziplay.xnxfyx), developed, published, and maintained by MANOO Tech ("we", "our", or "the Studio").

MuziPlay is an all-in-one Android media utility that features an ad-free streaming browser, high-speed universal media downloader, synchronized lyrics engine, and an offline studio-grade DSP audio player with Equalizer Pro and Smart Audio Isolation.

By installing, updating, or using MuziPlay, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy. If you do not agree with any terms, please do not install or use the application.

2. Privacy by Design & Zero Personal Data Sale

MuziPlay is engineered under a strict Privacy-by-Design philosophy. The application does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personal identification (such as name, email address, phone number, physical address, or payment details) to access its core features.

Zero Personal Data Sale: We do not collect, log, sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal identity, contact lists, GPS location, device contacts, or local audio files. We have never sold personal data to data brokers or third parties, and we never will.

3. Android Runtime Permissions & Technical Necessity

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policy requirements, MuziPlay requests only the minimum necessary runtime permissions strictly required to perform its media playback and download functions:

  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Required solely to discover, scan, and play your locally stored music tracks and display embedded album art inside the audio player. Your audio tracks remain strictly on your device; they are never uploaded, analyzed on cloud servers, or transmitted externally.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Required to maintain smooth, continuous audio playback when you navigate outside the app or when your phone screen is locked. It provides the persistent notification controls (Play, Pause, Skip, Previous).
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13 (API 33) and above to display playback status notifications, media controls, and download progress.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to enable user-initiated media streaming, fetch synchronized lyrics requested by the user, verify app updates via Google Play, and check network availability.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Required to prevent Android system sleep from interrupting continuous music playback while your screen is off.

4. Local Storage & On-Device Audio DSP Processing

MuziPlay incorporates high-fidelity audio engineering features, including Equalizer Pro, Bass Boost, 3D Spatial Audio, and Smart Audio Isolation (vocal filtering):

  • 100% On-Device Processing: All audio equalization, frequency processing, and sound isolation algorithms execute entirely within your device's local CPU/DSP hardware. None of your audio data is sent to external servers or AI cloud processors.
  • Playlists & Favorites: Your custom playlists, favorite tracks, playback history, and equalizer presets are stored strictly inside your local app sandbox.

5. Streaming Browser & Downloader Privacy

When using MuziPlay's streaming browser or downloading media:

  • Browsing Privacy: The in-app streaming browser connects directly between your device and the destination websites you choose to visit. MANOO Tech does not act as a proxy, nor do we monitor, inspect, log, or record your browsing URLs, search queries, or downloaded files.
  • Downloaded Content: Files downloaded via the app are saved directly to your device storage in the destination folder selected by you. We maintain no remote log or catalog of what you download.

6. Third-Party Services & Google Play SDKs

To deliver seamless app updates, license validation, and crash stability, MuziPlay integrates official Google developer tools:

  • Google Play Services: Used for app integrity, licensing, in-app updates, and security verification. Subject to the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Anonymized Diagnostics & Crash Reports: If an unforeseen app crash occurs, standard anonymous telemetry (such as device model, Android OS version, and stack trace logs) may be collected to help us resolve the crash. No personal data or user identifiers are included in these crash reports.
  • Advertising (if applicable): If an ad-supported version is deployed, Google advertising networks may serve contextual or standard ads in strict compliance with Google Play Developer Policies. You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID anytime via Android Settings > Google > Ads.

7. User Rights & Data Deletion Mechanism

In adherence to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and Google Play App Data Deletion requirements:

  • Local Data Removal: You can completely erase all local app data, preferences, playlists, and cached lyrics at any time simply by clearing app storage via Android Settings > Apps > MuziPlay > Clear Storage / Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application.
  • Support Communications Deletion: If you have ever emailed our support team and wish to have all correspondence, email records, and diagnostic logs permanently deleted, you may submit a request:
Submit a Deletion Request: Send an email with subject "Data Deletion Request - MuziPlay" to manootech2030@gmail.com. All associated email correspondence will be permanently purged within 48 business hours.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

MuziPlay is designed for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 within the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you are a parent or legal guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately at manootech2030@gmail.com, and we will promptly take steps to delete any such data.

9. Data Security & Encryption Standards

We prioritize user data protection through industry-standard technical safeguards:

  • Encryption in Transit: Any outgoing network requests initiated by MuziPlay (such as fetching lyrics or verifying updates) utilize secure cryptographic protocols (HTTPS / TLS 1.3).
  • Local Sandboxing: Android's application sandbox isolates MuziPlay from other apps on your device, preventing unauthorized third-party apps from accessing MuziPlay's internal files.
