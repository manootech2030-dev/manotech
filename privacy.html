Privacy Policy & Data Protection

At MANOO Tech, your privacy is fundamental to how we engineer our software. This policy outlines our commitment to transparency, data safety, and compliance across our studio web platforms and Google Play Android applications.

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all digital software products developed, operated, and published by MANOO Tech ("we", "our", or "the Studio"), including our official web platforms and all Android applications distributed via the Google Play Store (including MuziPlay, Brixy, and future titles).

By installing, accessing, or using any of our applications or web services, you acknowledge the terms described in this policy. We recommend reading this document carefully to understand how your privacy is protected.

2. Privacy by Design & Zero Personal Data Sale

MANOO Tech operates strictly under a Privacy-by-Design philosophy. We believe great software should serve users without intruding on their personal digital lives.

Our Core Promise: We do not collect, harvest, monetize, rent, or sell your personal identifiers, names, phone numbers, contact lists, GPS locations, or personal files to data brokers or third-party advertisers.

We build high-performance software, audio engines, and immersive casual games. Monetizing personal data is not, and will never be, part of our business model.

3. Device Permissions & On-Device Processing

Our mobile applications request only the minimum runtime permissions required to deliver their advertised functionality:

  • Local Storage & Media Access: Utilized exclusively on-device by media players (such as MuziPlay) to index and play back your local music tracks and display album artwork. Your media files are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Foreground Service: Used to maintain continuous audio playback and playback notification controls when you navigate away from the app.
  • Network Connectivity: Used strictly to fetch remote streaming media, check application updates, or download synchronized lyrics requested explicitly by you.
  • Haptic / Vibration Feedback: Used within our game engines (such as Brixy) to provide tactile feedback during gameplay.

4. Third-Party Services & Diagnostics

To ensure stability, prevent app crashes, and deliver updates, our applications may utilize standard SDKs provided by Google:

  • Google Play Services: Enables core platform features, app integrity verification, and in-app updates.
  • Performance Diagnostics & Crash Reports: Anonymous, aggregated diagnostic telemetry (such as device model, OS version, and stack traces) helps us identify and fix bugs rapidly.
  • Contextual Advertising: If an ad-supported version of a game or utility is deployed, standard Google advertising partners may serve contextual ads in accordance with Google Play Developer policies. You can reset your Google Advertising ID at any time via Android Device Settings.

5. Data Security & Storage Standards

We employ rigorous engineering safeguards. Any network communications initiated by our web platforms or applications use modern encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS 1.3).

We do not retain permanent user profiles on remote databases. Any technical records maintained by third-party diagnostic services are automatically rotated and purged in compliance with industry data retention standards.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting the privacy of children is of paramount concern to MANOO Tech. Our applications are engineered for general audiences and do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 within the European Economic Area).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately at manootech2030@gmail.com, and we will promptly purge such data from our systems.

7. User Rights & Data Deletion Requests

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and Google Play Developer Policies, all users possess fundamental rights regarding their digital data:

  • Right to Access: You may request a summary of any technical correspondence or diagnostic records associated with your email.
  • Right to Erasure (Data Deletion): You may request the irrevocable deletion of any email correspondence or support logs.
  • Right to Opt-Out: You can opt out of personalized advertisements by disabling interest-based ads in your Android device settings.
Submit a Deletion Request: Send an email with subject "Data Deletion Request" to manootech2030@gmail.com. Requests are confirmed and executed within 48 business hours.

8. Terms of Service & Intellectual Property

All applications, software architectures, logos, audio algorithms, game mechanics, and graphical assets published under MANOO Tech are protected by international copyright and intellectual property laws.

You are granted a non-exclusive, revocable, personal license to install and use our applications via official distribution channels (such as Google Play). You may not reverse-engineer, decompile, redistribute, or extract proprietary software modules without prior written consent from MANOO Tech.
